---
title: "Get contact info"
description: "A contact's data, with picture and WhatsApp Business profile."
openapi: "/api-reference/openapi.en.json GET /api/v1/contacts/{phone}"
---

Returns a contact's data: names, `username`, whether the account is Business
(`isBusiness`) or Official API (`isOfficial`), about text (`status`) and picture
(`avatar`). `POST /api/v1/contacts/getuser` returns the same response, with the
parameters in the body.

## WhatsApp Business profile

When the contact is a business account (WhatsApp Business app or Official API),
the response carries `data.businessProfile` with the public business profile:
description, email, address, websites, location, categories, business hours,
linked Instagram and Facebook accounts, offerings and cover photo.

The profile comes **by default**. It costs one extra query to WhatsApp (up to
10 s); if you don't need it, turn it off with `business=false` (query) on `GET`
or `"business": false` (body) on `POST`.

<Note>
  `businessProfile` only exists for business accounts and when the lookup
  succeeds. For a personal contact, with `business=false` or when WhatsApp does
  not answer in time, the key is **absent** — the rest of the contact arrives
  as usual. Treat the field as optional.
</Note>

### `businessProfile` fields

<ResponseField name="description" type="string">
  Business description
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="email" type="string">
  Public email
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="address" type="string">
  Address
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="websites" type="string[]">
  Registered websites (empty list when there are none). Some businesses put
  their Instagram link here instead of linking the account.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="location" type="object | null">
  `{ latitude, longitude }` of the location pinned on the profile, or `null`
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="categories" type="object[]">
  Business categories: `{ id, name }`
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="businessHours" type="object">
  <Expandable title="businessHours fields">
    <ResponseField name="timezone" type="string">
      Time zone of the business hours (e.g. `America/Sao_Paulo`)
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="days" type="object[]">
      One entry per time range. A split shift (e.g. morning and afternoon)
      produces **two entries for the same day**.
      <Expandable title="days fields">
        <ResponseField name="dayOfWeek" type="string">
          `mon` | `tue` | `wed` | `thu` | `fri` | `sat` | `sun`
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="mode" type="string">
          `specific_hours` (set hours) | `open_24h` (24 hours) |
          `appointment_only` (by appointment only)
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="openTime" type="string">
          Opening time as `HH:MM`. Empty unless `mode` is `specific_hours`
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="closeTime" type="string">
          Closing time as `HH:MM`. Empty unless `mode` is `specific_hours`
        </ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="instagram" type="object | null">
  Linked Instagram account: `{ handle, followers, url }`, or `null`
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="facebook" type="object | null">
  Linked Facebook page: `{ id, name, likes, url }`, or `null`
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="offerings" type="object[]">
  Services and amenities checked on the profile, grouped by category:
  `{ category, items: [{ id, name, offered }] }`
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="coverPhotoUrl" type="string">
  Cover photo. It is a signed WhatsApp CDN link that **expires** — download the
  image if you need to keep it.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="commerceExperience" type="string">
  `catalog` | `shop` | `none`
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="cartEnabled" type="boolean">
  Whether the shopping cart is enabled
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="accountType" type="string">
  `smb` (WhatsApp Business app) | `ent` (API / hosted account)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="verificationLevel" type="string">
  Verification level reported by WhatsApp
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="automatedType" type="string">
  Automation type reported by WhatsApp
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="memberSince" type="string">
  When the business signed up, in RFC 3339, or empty when WhatsApp does not say
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="memberSinceText" type="string">
  The same information as text, as WhatsApp displays it
</ResponseField>
