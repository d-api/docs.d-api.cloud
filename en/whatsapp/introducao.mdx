---
title: "WhatsApp (unofficial)"
description: "Connection via QR code or pairing code, organized by session."
---

The **unofficial WhatsApp** product connects a WhatsApp number to D-API via a
**QR code** or a **pairing code**. Each connection is a **session**,
identified by `sessionId`, which is present in every webhook.
